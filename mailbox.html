Northwind mailbox procedure

Every shared plan includes one mailbox per domain. The default address is

hello@ plus the primary domain.

To add a mailbox, open Email in the control panel, choose Create, and set a

local part and a password of at least 12 characters. The daily send cap is

500 messages per mailbox.

Webmail is Roundcube at webmail. plus the primary domain. IMAP and SMTP

use the same hostname as the primary domain, ports 993 and 465.
