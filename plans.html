Northwind Hosting plans

Northwind sells three shared plans and one unmanaged VPS.

  • Breeze: 1 site, 10 GB storage, 100 GB monthly transfer.
  • Gale: 10 sites, 50 GB storage, 500 GB monthly transfer.
  • Storm: unlimited sites on one account, 100 GB storage, 1 TB monthly transfer.
  • VPS: 2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 80 GB SSD, root access, no managed panel.

Shared plans include a control panel, nightly backups, and a mailbox. The VPS

does not include a panel or a mailbox; those are sold separately.
