Northwind backup retention

Northwind keeps nightly backups of each shared account for 14 days. The oldest

copy is discarded when a new night completes.

A restore is requested from Backups in the control panel. Shared restores run

within four hours during the business day. File-only restores are free; a

full-account restore may take the site offline for up to 30 minutes.

VPS disks are snapshotted weekly and kept for 7 days. The account holder

triggers a VPS rollback; Northwind does not roll back a VPS unasked.
