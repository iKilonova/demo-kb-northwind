Northwind refund policy

Northwind refunds a new shared plan within 30 days of the first invoice if the

account holder asks in writing and has not used more than half of the plan's

monthly transfer.

VPS plans are not refundable after the first 48 hours. Domain registrations

and TLS certificates are never refundable.

A refund returns the unused prepaid balance to the original payment method

within ten business days. Hosting is cancelled at the moment the refund is

approved.
